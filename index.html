🔔 FloatingNotify

Privacy Policy

✅ No personal data collected

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the FloatingNotify application for Android and Wear OS devices, developed by Sheriff79 (the "Service Provider").

FloatingNotify is a commercial application that forwards phone notifications as animated floating icons to a paired Wear OS smartwatch. The app operates entirely locally via Bluetooth and does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Information Collection and Use

FloatingNotify does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. Specifically:

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No personal information is collected (name, email, location, etc.)
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated
  • Notification content is processed locally on your device only and is never sent to any server

Permissions Used

The application requests the following permissions, used solely to provide core functionality:

  • Notification Listener Service — to detect incoming notifications on your phone and forward them to your Wear OS watch as floating icons
  • Accessibility Service — to display floating overlay icons on the Wear OS watch screen above other applications
  • System Alert Window — to render floating notification icons as overlay on the watch display
  • Wake Lock — to keep the service running when the screen is off
  • Receive Boot Completed — to automatically start the service after device reboot
  • Schedule Exact Alarm — to support time-based scheduling of notification display
  • Query All Packages — to display the list of installed apps for user configuration

None of these permissions are used to collect or share personal data.

Third-Party Services

FloatingNotify uses Google Play Services and Google Wearable API to communicate between the phone and the Wear OS watch via Bluetooth. These services are provided by Google LLC and are subject to Google's own privacy policy.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks are used.

Data Storage

All user preferences and settings (selected apps, physics parameters, schedule) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the Service Provider.

Data Deletion

Since no personal data is collected or stored on external servers, there is no data to delete remotely.

To delete all local app data:

  • Go to Android Settings → Apps → FloatingNotify → Storage → Clear Data
  • Or simply uninstall the application

This will remove all locally stored preferences and settings.

Children's Privacy

FloatingNotify is not directed at children under the age of 16. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided any information through this application, please contact us immediately.
